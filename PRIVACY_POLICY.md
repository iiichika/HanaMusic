# Privacy Policy for Hana Music / Hana Music 隐私政策

**Effective Date / 生效日期：** November 13, 2026 (2026年11月13日)

Thank you for using **Hana Music** ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Hana Music handles your information when you use the App on iPhone and iPad.

感谢您使用 **Hana Music**（以下简称“本应用”）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明您在 iPhone 和 iPad 上使用本应用时，我们如何处理相关信息。

---

## 1. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

**Hana Music does not collect, upload, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal information.**
**Hana Music 不会收集、上传、在外部服务器存储、出售或共享您的任何个人信息。**

* **No Account Required / 无需注册账号：** You do not need to create an account or log in to use the App. / 您无需注册或登录账号即可使用本应用。
* **No Analytics or Tracking / 无统计追踪与广告：** The App does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or cross-app tracking technologies. / 本应用未集成任何第三方数据分析 SDK、广告网络或跨应用追踪技术。

---

## 2. On-Device Permissions & Local Data / 设备权限与本地数据使用

All audio files, library metadata, and preferences are processed **strictly locally on your device**:
所有音频文件、媒体库信息及应用偏好设置均**仅在您的设备本地处理**：

* **Local Audio Files / 本地音频文件：** Audio files you import via the system Files app or document picker are stored solely within your device's local app sandbox (`On My iPhone` / `On My iPad`) for playback and management. We cannot access these files remotely.
  您通过系统“文件”应用或文件选择器导入的音频文件，仅保存在您设备本地的应用沙盒目录（“我的 iPhone” / “我的 iPad”）中用于播放与管理，开发者无法远程访问这些文件。
* **Apple Music & Media Library Access (`MPMediaLibrary`) / 系统媒体资料库权限：** Permission to access your device's Media Library is requested only when you actively choose to view or play tracks already synced to your device's Music library. Library metadata and playback are handled exclusively on-device via Apple's system frameworks and are never transmitted to any external server. You may decline or revoke this permission at any time in **iOS/iPadOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Media & Apple Music**, and local audio import and playback will continue to work normally.
  仅当您主动切换至系统媒体资料库或触发同步时，本应用才会请求访问您设备“音乐”资料库的权限。资料库曲目读取与播放完全通过 Apple 系统框架在本地完成，绝不会上传至任何外部服务器。您可随时在系统 **设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 媒体与 Apple Music** 中拒绝或撤回该权限，拒绝后本地音频导入与播放功能仍可正常使用。
* **App Preferences (`UserDefaults`) / 应用偏好设置：** Your playback preferences, interface settings, and locally created playlists are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage.
  您的播放偏好、界面设置及本地创建的歌单仅通过系统标准本地存储保存在您的设备上。

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion / 数据保留与删除

Because Hana Music does not store your data on external servers, all data remains under your control on your device. You can delete imported audio files or playlists directly within the App, or remove all locally stored app data by uninstalling Hana Music.

由于本应用不在任何外部服务器保存您的数据，所有数据均完全由您在设备本地掌控。您可以直接在应用内删除导入的音频或歌单，或通过卸载本应用清除全部本地数据。

---

## 4. Children's Privacy / 儿童隐私

Hana Music does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

本应用不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童（或您所在司法管辖区规定的相应法定年龄以下未成年人）的任何个人信息。
